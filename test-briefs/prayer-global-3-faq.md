# Prayer.Global 3: FAQ page (from an idea)

**Prompt:** We need a FAQ page for Prayer.Global. Ask me what you need.

**If asked:**
- 10 questions in 3 groups: Getting started, Using the map, Privacy. Draft them from what you know of Prayer.Global and mark them as draft.
- Each answer is 1–3 sentences; some link to other pages (/about/, /privacy/, the app stores).
- A search box isn't needed. A "Still have questions?" callout at the end with a contact link → /contact/.

**Watch for:** `<details>`/`<summary>` (or headings) instead of a JS accordion, group headings at the right level, no new block for something the topic-links or callout band already does.
