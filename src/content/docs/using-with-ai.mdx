---
title: Using this with AI
description: How to get on-brand output from an AI assistant.
---

Every guide has an **AI brief**: a single markdown file containing the exact values and rules for that brand.

## Quick start

1. Open the guide you need and go to its **For AI** page.
2. Click **Copy brief to clipboard**.
3. Paste it at the start of a chat with your AI assistant, then ask for what you need.

## Example prompts

> Here is our brand brief. Build a one-page sign-up site for our youth event. Use the CSS variables from the brief, don't hard-code colours.

> Here is our brand brief. Review this page I've made and list anything that breaks the rules.

> Here is our brand brief. Write the intro paragraph for our newsletter in the brand voice.

## For developers and AI tools

- `/llms.txt` lists every guide.
- `/guides/<name>.md` is the brief for one guide.
- `/tokens/<name>.css` is a drop-in stylesheet of CSS variables.
