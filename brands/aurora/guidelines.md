## Voice

Warm, plain-spoken, encouraging. Short sentences. Speak directly to the reader.

## Principles

- **Welcoming first.** Every page should feel like an invitation, not a form.
- **One clear next step.** Each screen has a single primary action.
- **Room to breathe.** Generous white space; nothing feels crammed.

## Do

- Use primary for the single most important action on a screen.
- Use generous white space; keep body text under 70 characters per line.
- Use Fraunces for headings and Inter for everything else.
- Keep corners rounded using the radius tokens.

## Don't

- Don't use primary and secondary together in the same button group.
- Don't put muted text on coloured backgrounds.
- Don't use more than two font families.
- Don't use sharp (0px) corners.
